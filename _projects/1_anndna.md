A knowledge-distillation framework that injects genomic annotation signals into a semi-supervised genomic language model, producing representations that are more sensitive to functional context. Distilling annotation knowledge into the student model improves downstream variant-effect prediction over the base model alone.

This work was presented at the ICML 2026 GenBio Workshop (Spotlight) {% cite sim2026anndna %}.
