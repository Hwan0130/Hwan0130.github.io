A survey and analysis of genomic language models, examining how architectural choices shape variant-effect interpretation. The work identifies the "context distribution problem" — a systematic mismatch between the sequence-context distributions models are trained on and those encountered at inference — and discusses its implications for reliable variant interpretation.

Currently under review at *Experimental & Molecular Medicine* {% cite sim2026genomiclm %}.
