Using genomic foundation models to systematically mutate the CD19 enhancer in silico, this project nominates functional variants and characterizes cell-type-specific regulatory activity at the locus. The approach extends naturally to in silico MPRA screens of psychiatric-disorder GWAS variants, prioritizing candidates for downstream experimental validation.
