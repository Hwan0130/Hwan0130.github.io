Deep learning-guided design of cell-type-specific synthetic enhancers targeting *MEF2C*, a gene whose regulatory landscape shows autism-specific patterns. The goal is to generate sequences with predictable, cell-type-restricted regulatory activity that can be tested experimentally to probe ASD-relevant gene regulation.
