Deep learning models that analyze whole-genome de novo variants from large-scale autism cohorts (SSC, MSSNG, SFARI, and Korean cohorts) to identify subtypes of autism spectrum disorder and predict associated phenotypes, linking distinct regulatory architectures and variant burden to clinical presentation.
